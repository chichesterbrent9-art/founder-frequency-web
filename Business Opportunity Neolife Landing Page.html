01 — What It Is

NeoLife, Plainly

NeoLife is a direct selling company built around high-quality nutritional and wellness products, backed by more than 68 years in business and a presence in over 50 countries. Promoters earn in two ways: selling products directly to customers, and optionally building and supporting a team of other Promoters — the standard structure across the direct selling industry.

Every product is developed under the guidance of NeoLife's Scientific Advisory Board, an international network of scientists and researchers whose work has appeared in respected peer-reviewed nutrition journals.

This isn't a course, a piece of software, or a passive-income product. It's a real small business you run, with real customer relationships and real effort behind any results.

02 — How It Works

The Business Model

Product Sales

You buy products at a Promoter price and sell to customers, earning the margin. This is the most direct and immediate way to earn as a new Promoter.

Team Building

Optionally, you can recruit and support other Promoters. Earnings here scale with your team's collective sales, not simply the number of people you sign up.

05 — FAQ

Common Questions

Is this a pyramid scheme?
NeoLife is a direct selling company — earnings are tied to actual product sales, not just recruitment fees. That said, do your own diligence on any opportunity: check for a genuine product with real end-consumer demand, real customers outside the Promoter network, and a published income disclosure.
What does it cost to start?
Enrollment is $75 USD ($98 CDN), with a $49 USD ($64 CDN) annual renewal after your first year. Your enrollment order also needs to total at least 30 PV.
What if I don't want to recruit, just sell product?
That's a valid way to participate — plenty of Promoters focus purely on retail sales without building a team.
How much time does this take?
It depends on the income you're building toward — there's no single number that's honest for everyone. Part of your onboarding is a Game Plan Interview where you land on a weekly time commitment that's realistic for your own goals, rather than a one-size-fits-all promise.
